# 📊 Customer Churn & Retention Analysis (Task 2)

## 🔍 Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer churn behavior using a telecom dataset to identify key drivers of customer attrition and provide actionable business insights.

---

## 📌 Key Metrics

* Total Customers: 7,032
* Churn Rate: 26.58%
* Churned Customers: 1,869
* Average Tenure: 32.42 months

---

## 📊 Dashboard Insights

### 🔹 Churn Distribution

Approximately 1 in 4 customers churned, indicating a significant retention challenge.

### 🔹 Key Drivers of Churn

* Month-to-month contracts have the highest churn
* Customers with short tenure (0–3 months) are most likely to leave
* Lack of tech support increases churn risk
* Fiber optic users show higher churn compared to DSL

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Encourage long-term contracts through incentives
* Improve onboarding experience for new customers
* Promote value-added services like tech support
* Identify high-risk customers early for retention campaigns
